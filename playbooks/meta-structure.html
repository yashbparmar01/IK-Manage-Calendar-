Meta structure playbook

ElementWhat it isWhere it belongs
Business portfolioThe container that owns Meta assets on behalf of a company, and the only place access is granted.One per company — ours, and one per client
FB PageThe brand's presence on Facebook, and what an Instagram professional account attaches to.The brand's own portfolio
Insta professional accountA Business or Creator account, linked to the Page; only a Business account can be read by tools.The brand's own portfolio
Ad accountWhere campaigns are built and money is spent; the invoice is raised to whoever owns it.Whoever pays — see the billing rule below
Pixel and datasetThe code on a website that records what visitors do, and the store those events go into.The brand's own portfolio — history is lost if it moves later
DomainA website verified as belonging to a business, which unlocks link editing and reporting.One portfolio worldwide — it cannot be verified twice
CatalogueThe product feed behind shopping and dynamic ads.The brand's own portfolio
WhatsApp Business accountA phone number registered for business messaging and campaign click-throughs.The brand's own portfolio
Payment methodA card, a bank mandate or prepaid funds attached to an ad account; separate from ownership.On the paying party's own ad account, never shared across brands
Partner accessOne portfolio granting another named access to named assets. This is how we reach client work.Granted by the client, to us
PeopleIndividuals added to a portfolio one at a time — the client's own staff in their portfolio, our staff in ours.Each person in their own employer's portfolio only
PartnersWhole businesses granted access rather than individuals — us in the client's portfolio, and any other agency or media buyer they work with.Listed under Partners, never under People
Customer list custom audienceContact data the client gives us — phone numbers, emails — uploaded to build a targetable audience. It is the client's data, not ours.Uploaded into the client's own ad account, never ours
Website and engagement custom audienceAn audience built from pixel events or from people who interacted with the Page, without any file being uploaded.The client's own ad account, tied to their pixel
Lookalike audienceA new audience Meta builds by finding people who resemble a source audience.The same ad account as its source audience
Custom Audience TermsThe agreement accepted once per ad account, confirming the uploader has the right to use that contact data.Accepted by the client's ad account, by a named person
Audience sharingOne ad account lending an audience to another. It makes one client's contact data usable by another advertiser.Off, unless the client has asked for it in writing
Lead formThe instant form inside an ad that collects a prospect's details, and the leads it stores.The client's own Page and ad account
Asset groupA bundle of one brand's assets, so a person is given a brand rather than a list of items.One per brand, inside the portfolio that owns it
System userA non-human account that carries a tool's access; it has no password and does not leave.Our portfolio — one per tool
Business verificationMeta confirming a portfolio is a real registered company, using its incorporation documents.Every portfolio, ours included
Two-factor authenticationA second check at sign-in, set as a requirement by the portfolio rather than by each person.Required of everyone, everywhere
